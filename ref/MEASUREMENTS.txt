Cybertruck side-elevation measurements
=======================================

Primary reference (authoritative outline):
  Page: https://www.dimensions.com/element/tesla-cybertruck
  SVG:  https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5b44edefca321a1e2d0c2aa6/5f45a0bed5243d00d558c6c9_Dimensions-Transport-Tesla-Cybertruck-Dimensions.svg
  Local: ref/cyber-dimensions.svg, ref/side_elev.png

ORIENTATION (important):
  The Dimensions.com side elevation already FACES RIGHT.
  LEFT  = REAR  = tall, nearly vertical tailgate + bed + rear bumper.
  RIGHT = FRONT = pointed nose, windshield, light-bar face.
  Do not mirror this drawing to make a right-facing sprite.

  (A previous write-up swapped front/rear. The 44%-from-front peak
  figure was correct once the right-hand end is treated as the nose.)

Measured on the side view (pink ink), body bbox:
  Length L = 1179 px
  Height H = 374 px  (roof peak to tire contact)
  L/H      = 3.152   (tesla.com production 223.7" / 70.6" = 3.169;
                      drawing labels 231.7" / 75" disagree with the ink)

  Peak x: 56% of L from the REAR (left), 44% of L from the FRONT (right).
  Sail  (peak -> tailgate): 658 px run, drop 92 px, ~8.0°  — the LONG, SHALLOW plane.
  Hood  (peak -> nose):     520 px run, drop 175 px, ~18.6° — the SHORT, STEEPER plane.
  Tailgate top: 75% of H, nearly vertical.
  Nose tip top: 53% of H (light bar); front face down to ~36% of H.
  Character line: ~35% of H.
  Rocker / ground clearance: ~16% of H.
  Wheel diameter: ~16% of L  (~50% of H).
  Wheelbase: ~64% of L  (149.9" / 231.7" labeled, 149.9" / 223.7" production).
  Front overhang (right): ~15.5% of L.
  Rear overhang  (left):  ~20.1% of L.
  C-pillar / cab-bed break: ~35% from the rear (bed is the last third).
  B-pillar: just behind the peak.
  A-pillar: on the windshield, ~73% from the rear.

Sprite:
  104 px long × 33 px to the peak (104/33 = 3.15).
  Peak at x=58.  Wheels r=8 (17 px diameter, 16.3% of L) at x=21 and x=88.
  Outline derived from the elevation scaled to this grid; shading is original
  (stainless greys, black cladding, light bar). The photo/SVG is not embedded.
