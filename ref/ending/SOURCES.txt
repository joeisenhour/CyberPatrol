Ending scene references (photos are NOT embedded in the game; pixel art is original)

Starship (Human Landing System, landed on legs — chosen because Super Heavy
does not go to the lunar surface). Official dimensions: ~50 m tall, 9 m
diameter (NASA). Stainless ogive-nosed cylinder, window band, NASA marking,
black equatorial band, black engine skirt, four landing legs. No flaps
(HLS configuration).
