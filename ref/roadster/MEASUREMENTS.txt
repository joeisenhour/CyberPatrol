Tesla Roadster (next-gen) — side-profile measurements
=====================================================

Version used:
  Official Ano White studio photographs from the Tesla Roadster
  unveil, 15–16 October 2026, SpaceX McGregor, Texas (production
  targeted 2028). This is the newly unveiled wedge / “cyberfied”
  design (SR-71 / F-35 inspired), NOT the 2017 red concept.

  No dimensioned orthographic elevation was published. The primary
  reference is Tesla’s official nearly side-on studio photograph
  (doors closed), the closest true side profile available.

Photo (doors closed, nose on the LEFT in the official still):
  Length L ≈ 1000 px (black front splitter to rear bumper)
  Height H ≈ 256 px (roof peak to tire contact)
  L/H      ≈ 3.91   (very low, long wedge — supercar proportions)
  Peak: 50.8% of L from the front (almost mid-car, over the cabin)
  Wheel diameter ≈ 166 px (16.6% of L, ~65% of H — large for a low body)
  Wheelbase ≈ 599 px (59.9% of L)
  Front overhang ≈ 12.9% of L
  Rear overhang  ≈ 10.6% of L
  Ground clearance is very low (splitter / rocker close to the ground)
  Nose: sharp, nearly vertical hidden-light-bar face, black splitter shelf
  Canopy: long gold-glazed glass from behind the front axle to a C-pillar
  Tail: Kamm cut, nearly vertical, thin red rear-light slash, black diffuser
  Lower body: black skirt / rocker, rising slightly toward the rear
  Wheels: 5 thin dark star-spokes, light rim, dark hub

Sprite (game, facing RIGHT, photo flipped):
  96 px long × 25 px to the peak (96/25 = 3.84, closest integer fit)
  Peak at x=47 (49% from the rear)
  Wheels r=8 (16 px Ø) at x=19 (rear) and x=76 (front)
  Outline derived from the official photo scaled to this grid;
  shading is original (Ano White, gold glass, black skirt, light bar).
  The photograph is not embedded in the game.

Alignment after the final sprite (facing right):
  Roof MAE = 0.21 px over cabin and hood (x=2–90), 99% of those
  columns within 1 px, versus the official photo scaled to 96 px long.
  Wheels: photo maps to cx≈18.0 / 75.6, r≈8.0; sprite uses 19 / 76, r=8.
  Wheelbase 57/96 = 59.4% (photo 59.9%). Front overhang 12.5% (photo 12.9%).
  Rear overhang 11.5% (photo 10.6%) — slightly long at this grid.
  Peak at x=47 is 51% from the front (photo 50.8%).
  L/H sprite 96/25 = 3.84 vs photo 3.91 (closest integer fit; the sprite
  is ~0.4 px taller when lengths are locked).
  The official nearly-vertical light-bar face sits at about x=93; the
  sprite carries a 2–3 px blunt face to x=96 so the light bar and
  splitter still read at 96 px. No dimensioned elevation exists; all
  ratios are from the official studio photograph, not a blueprint.

  Front overhang 11/78 = 14.1% (photo 12.9%); rear 9/78 = 11.5% (photo 10.6%).
